# EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT - FOREIGN DOMESTIC HELPER
Hong Kong SAR

This contract is made on 5 October 2026 between:

Employer: Mr. Wong Chi Keung of Lantau Bay Household, Flat 12C, Block 3, Discovery Bay, Lantau Island, Hong Kong (the "Employer").

Helper: Maricel Dela Cruz, Passport No. P5520917, a citizen of the Philippines (the "Helper").

Introduced by: Island Care Employment Agency, licence no. 51-4421 (the "Agency").

## Terms of employment

1. Position: live-in domestic helper at the Employer's home. Duties are cleaning, laundry, cooking and looking after an elderly parent. The Helper will not work at any other address.

2. Duration: two years from 20 October 2026.

3. Wage: HK$4,800 per month, paid on the last day of each month in cash.

4. Food: the Employer gives the Helper a food allowance of HK$900 per month instead of providing meals.

5. Working hours: the Helper is on duty from 6 a.m. until the family goes to bed, usually 11 p.m., and may be called at any time during the night.

6. Rest days: the Helper has 2 rest days per month, which the Employer chooses. Other rest days are paid at the daily rate if the Helper works.

7. Statutory holidays and leave: the Helper receives statutory holidays and paid annual leave only if the Employer agrees on each occasion.

8. Agency fee: the Helper will pay the Agency a placement fee of HK$21,000, by deduction of HK$3,500 from each of the first 6 monthly wages.

9. Passport and documents: the Employer will keep the Helper's passport, identity card and employment visa in a safe place at home.

10. Damage: the Employer may deduct the full cost from wages for any item broken or lost by the Helper.

11. Mobile phone: the Helper may use a mobile phone only with the Employer's permission.

12. Housing: the Helper sleeps in the storeroom next to the kitchen. The Helper may not sleep or stay outside the Employer's home.

13. Termination: either side may end this contract by giving 7 days' notice.

14. Return passage: the Helper pays for her own return flight to the Philippines when the contract ends.

15. Governing law: this contract is governed by the Employment Ordinance of Hong Kong.

Signed: Wong Chi Keung (Employer)    Maricel Dela Cruz (Helper)
Date: 5 October 2026
