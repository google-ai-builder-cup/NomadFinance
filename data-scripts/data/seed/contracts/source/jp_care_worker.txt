# EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT - CARE WORKER
Sakura Gardens Care Co., Ltd., Saitama Prefecture, Japan

This contract is made on 28 August 2026 between:

Employer: Sakura Gardens Care Co., Ltd., 3-12-5 Kitaurawa, Urawa-ku, Saitama-shi, Saitama (the "Employer").

Employee: Miguel Santos, Passport No. P3306641, a citizen of the Philippines, residence status Specified Skilled Worker (Nursing Care) (the "Employee").

## Terms of employment

1. Job: care worker at the Employer's nursing care facility "Sakura Gardens Urawa", helping residents with meals, bathing, moving and daily activities.

2. Period: 5 years from 1 October 2026, subject to renewal of the residence status.

3. Place of work: Sakura Gardens Urawa, Saitama-shi. The Employer may move the Employee to another of its facilities in Saitama Prefecture with 30 days' notice.

4. Working hours: 8 hours a day and 40 hours a week on a rotating shift pattern, including some night shifts, with a 60-minute break. Overtime needs the Employee's agreement within the written agreement filed with the labor standards office.

5. Wage: JPY 215,000 per month basic wage, which is not less than the minimum wage of Saitama Prefecture, paid on the 25th of each month by bank transfer. The Employer will not pay wages in kind.

6. Overtime and night work: overtime is paid at 125% of the hourly wage and night work between 10 p.m. and 5 a.m. at an extra 25%, as required by the Labor Standards Act.

7. Days off: at least one day off a week, and 10 days of paid annual leave after 6 months of service.

8. Deductions: the Employer deducts health insurance, pension, employment insurance and income tax as required by law. It also deducts JPY 45,000 per month for the company dormitory and JPY 20,000 per month as a support service fee.

9. Residence card: to avoid loss, the Employer's general affairs office keeps the Employee's residence card and passport, and hands them over on request for official procedures.

10. Early resignation: if the Employee resigns within 3 years of the start date, the Employee will pay the Employer JPY 300,000 as a training cost compensation.

11. Termination: the Employer gives 30 days' notice, or 30 days of wages in place of notice, before dismissing the Employee. The Employee asks to leave by giving 30 days' notice.

12. Insurance: the Employee is enrolled in workers' accident compensation insurance, employment insurance, health insurance and the welfare pension.

13. Support: the Employer will provide Japanese language study time of 2 hours a week and a consultation line in Tagalog.

14. Governing law: Japanese law applies. A Tagalog translation of this contract has been given to the Employee.

Signed: Hiroshi Tanabe (Employer)    Miguel Santos (Employee)
Date: 28 August 2026
