## Terms of employment

1. Position: casual fruit picker and packer on the Employer's farm in the Mildura district. This is casual employment and no minimum number of hours is guaranteed.

2. Start date: 5 October 2026, for the 2026-27 harvest season to about 31 March 2027.

3. Pay rate: AUD 22.00 per hour, paid weekly into the Employee's bank account. This rate includes any casual loading and any penalty rates.

4. Hours: the Employee will normally work 38 hours a week, with extra hours when the Employer asks. The Employee may refuse extra hours that are unreasonable.

5. Superannuation: the hourly rate above is inclusive of superannuation, and the Employer will not make any separate superannuation payment.

6. Agency processing fee: the Employee will pay the Agency a processing fee of AUD 2,500 for finding this job, collected by the Employer from the Employee's first 5 weekly pay packets, AUD 500 each.

7. Accommodation: the Employer provides a bed in a shared farm cabin and deducts AUD 250 per week. The Employee agrees to this deduction in writing by signing here.

8. Transport: the Employer provides a shuttle from the cabins to the orchard. A charge of AUD 40 per week is deducted from pay for the shuttle.

9. Passport: the Employee keeps his own passport. The Employer will make a copy of the passport and visa to check work rights.

10. Breaks and public holidays: the Employee gets a 30-minute meal break after 5 hours of work. Public holidays are unpaid unless the Employee works.

11. Termination: casual employment can be ended by either side at any time without notice. The Employer will pay all wages owed on the next pay day after the end of work.

12. Workers' compensation: the Employer holds a workers' compensation policy that covers the Employee at work.

13. Fair Work: the Employee has been given the Fair Work Information Statement and may contact the Fair Work Ombudsman at any time.

14. Governing law: this agreement is governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia and the Fair Work Act 2009.
