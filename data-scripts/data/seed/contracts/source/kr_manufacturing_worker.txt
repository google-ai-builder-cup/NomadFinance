# STANDARD EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT - MANUFACTURING WORKER
Han River Metals Co., Ltd., Siheung-si, Gyeonggi-do, Republic of Korea

This contract is made on 21 August 2026 between:

Employer: Han River Metals Co., Ltd., 78 Jeongwang-daero, Siheung-si, Gyeonggi-do, represented by Mr. Park Dong-Seok (the "Company").

Employee: Rajesh Kumar, Passport No. R7714203, a citizen of India, E-9 non-professional employment visa (the "Worker").

## Terms of employment

1. Duration: 36 months from 1 September 2026, under the Employment Permit System. The contract renews only if both sides agree in writing.

2. Job: operator of metal pressing machines at the Company's factory in Siheung-si.

3. Wage: KRW 1,950,000 per month, paid on the 25th of each month by bank transfer. This figure is the basic wage and includes all allowances.

4. Working hours: 10 hours a day, 6 days a week, Monday to Saturday, with a 30-minute meal break. The Company may extend the hours further when orders are heavy and the Worker must comply.

5. Overtime: hours beyond the normal hours are paid at the same hourly rate as normal work, because the monthly wage already includes overtime.

6. Rest days: Sunday is the rest day. Public holidays are working days unless the Company announces otherwise.

7. Passport: for safekeeping, the Company keeps the Worker's passport and alien registration card in the office safe for the whole contract period and returns them at the end of the contract.

8. Recruitment fee: the Worker will pay the placement agency KRW 1,800,000 for arranging this job, by deduction of KRW 150,000 from each of the first 12 monthly wages.

9. Dormitory: the Company provides a dormitory room shared with five other workers. The Company deducts KRW 400,000 per month for the room, whether or not the Worker uses it.

10. Penalty for leaving: if the Worker leaves the Company before the end of the contract for any reason, the Worker will pay the Company a penalty of KRW 2,000,000.

11. Termination: the Company may end this contract at any time, without notice and without giving a reason, at its sole discretion.

12. Insurance: the Company enrols the Worker in industrial accident insurance, national health insurance, employment insurance and the departure guarantee insurance as required by law.

13. Return: the Worker pays for his own flight home at the end of the contract.

14. Governing law: this contract is governed by the Labor Standards Act and the Act on Foreign Workers' Employment of the Republic of Korea. The Korean language version is the official one.
