# EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT - FOREIGN DOMESTIC WORKER
Singapore

This contract is made on 2 September 2026 between:

Employer: Mrs. Lim Hui Min, NRIC S7412345A, of the Example Household, Block 214 Bishan Street 23, #08-112, Singapore 570214 (the "Employer").

Employee: Priya Sharma, Passport No. P9081726, a citizen of India (the "Employee").

## Terms of employment

1. Position and duties: the Employee will work as a live-in domestic worker at the Employer's home address above. Duties are housework, cooking, marketing and care of the Employer's two children aged 6 and 9. The Employee will not be asked to work at any other address.

2. Duration: 24 months, starting on 15 September 2026, which is the validity period of the Employee's work permit.

3. Salary: SGD 650 per month, as declared to the Ministry of Manpower. The salary is paid into the Employee's own bank account on or before the 7th day after the end of each month, together with an itemised payslip.

4. Rest days: the Employee has one rest day every week, normally Sunday, from 9 a.m. to 9 p.m. The Employer may ask the Employee to work on a rest day only by agreement, and will then pay at the Employee's daily rate or give another day off, at the Employer's choice.

5. Daily rest: the Employee will have at least 8 hours of continuous rest every day and a break for each meal.

6. Public holidays and leave: the Employee gets the public holidays as rest days. After 3 months of service the Employee gets 7 days of paid annual leave a year, increasing by one day for each further year of service.

7. Food and housing: the Employer provides three meals a day and a room with a bed and a lockable cupboard. The Employer will not deduct any amount from the salary for food or housing.

8. Medical care: the Employer will pay for all medical treatment, buys the medical insurance and personal accident insurance required by the Ministry of Manpower, and will pay for the six-monthly medical examination.

9. Work permit and documents: the Employee keeps her own passport, work permit card and other personal documents at all times. The Employer will not hold them.

10. Recruitment fees: the Employee has paid no fee to the Employer. Any fee charged by a recruitment agency is limited to the amount allowed by Singapore law and is not deducted from the Employee's salary by the Employer.

11. Communication: the Employee may keep her own mobile phone and may use it to call her family in her free time.

12. Notice: either side may end this contract by giving one month's written notice, or one month's salary in place of notice. The Employer may end the contract without notice only for serious misconduct, and must first pay all salary owed.

13. Return home: when the contract ends for any reason the Employer will pay for the Employee's air ticket back to India and will pay all salary owed before she leaves.

14. Disputes: the Employee may talk to the Ministry of Manpower, her embassy or a non-governmental organisation at any time. This contract is governed by the laws of Singapore.
