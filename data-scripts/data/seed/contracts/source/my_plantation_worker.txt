# EMPLOYMENT CONTRACT - PLANTATION WORKER
Sawit Jaya Plantations Sdn. Bhd., Segamat, Johor, Malaysia

This contract is made on 10 September 2026 between:

Employer: Sawit Jaya Plantations Sdn. Bhd., Company No. 201801234567, Ladang Sawit Jaya, 85000 Segamat, Johor (the "Employer").

Employee: Agus Pratama, Passport No. C4417829, a citizen of Indonesia, Temporary Employment Visit Pass (Plantation sector) (the "Employee").

Recruited through: Mitra Karya Tenaga Sdn. Bhd., a licensed private employment agency (the "Agency").

## Terms of employment

1. Position: harvester and general plantation worker on the Employer's oil palm estate.

2. Duration: 24 months from 1 October 2026, renewable by agreement.

3. Wage: RM1,700 per month basic wage, which is the national minimum wage, paid by bank transfer within 7 days after the end of each month. The Employee will also receive a harvesting incentive of RM0.10 per fresh fruit bunch above 600 bunches a month.

4. Working hours: 8 hours a day, 45 hours a week, Monday to Saturday. The Employee has a 30-minute break after every 5 hours of work.

5. Rest days and holidays: one whole rest day every week, normally Sunday. The Employee has 11 paid public holidays each year and 8 days of paid annual leave in the first year.

6. Overtime: work beyond normal hours is voluntary and is paid at the normal hourly rate, with no extra premium.

7. Passport: the Employer keeps the Employee's passport in the estate office for the whole contract and gives it back when the Employee leaves Malaysia.

8. Placement fee: the Employee pays the Agency a placement fee equal to one month's basic wage, RM1,700, by one deduction from the first monthly wage.

9. Housing: the Employer provides a room in the estate quarters with water and electricity, and deducts RM150 per month for housing. The Employer will apply for any permission needed for this deduction.

10. Medical and injury: the Employer registers the Employee with the Social Security Organisation for work injury cover and buys the foreign workers' hospitalisation and health insurance.

11. Sick leave: the Employee gets paid sick leave after examination by a doctor chosen and paid for by the Employer.

12. Notice: either side may end this contract by giving 2 weeks' written notice.

13. Transport home: at the end of the contract the Employer pays the Employee's bus fare to the airport and the Employee pays the air ticket.

14. Complaints: the Employee may complain to the Labour Department, the Indonesian consulate or a trade union. This contract is governed by the Employment Act 1955 of Malaysia.
