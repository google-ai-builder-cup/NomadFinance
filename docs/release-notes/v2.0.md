## What shipped

Two modules, both behind feature flags (`config/feature_flags`), off for everyone unless switched on:

- **Scam Analyzer** (`scam`): a pasted message, a screenshot or a shared one gets a verdict, the tricks used, plain next steps with read-aloud, a history, and a one-tap anonymous report to Exploitation Radar.
- **Financial Coach** (`coach`): a short interview in the worker's own language, a preparedness score from 0 to 100, a 13-week plan with a PDF in six languages, and check-ins at day 30, 60 and 90.

Around them: the `/api/v2` surface (standard error envelope, streamed replies, idempotency keys), PII redaction, log-based metrics, a dashboard and alerts, a per-feature daily Gemini budget that degrades to "try again later", shared Coach and Scam signals for Financial Identity, a first-run tour, and Coach funnel analytics. 90 commits in 78 pull requests (#108 to #185) are in the changelog, including the hardening and regression work of NF-HR-004 to -010 and the documentation, analytics and regression suites (NF-HR-005, -006, -012, -013, -015) that merged after the first changelog (which stopped at #178).
